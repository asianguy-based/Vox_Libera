Free Speech, For Everyone

A free AAC app that gives anyone who has difficulty speaking a voice.
Vox Libera is a free AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) web app built for anyone who has difficulty speaking — due to aphasia, autism, cerebral palsy, ALS, stroke, or any other condition. Users build sentences from customizable word and picture cards and have them spoken aloud instantly, using their device's own built-in text-to-speech voices — completely free, private, and fully functional offline. There's no cost, no account sign-up, no ads, and no API keys required.
Tap-to-speak word & picture cards, fully customizable
Natural text-to-speech using your device's own voices — works offline
Full-screen "big text" mode for showing sentences to others
Personal voice recordings for instant playback
Emergency & caregiver info stored right in the app
Installs like a real app — no app-store fees, ever
